# Sugargoo Spreadsheet Guide: Multi-Platform Buying and Consolidated Shipping Research

This guide is written for international shoppers comparing multi-platform discovery with consolidated-parcel and cost-research questions. It uses sampled community discussions as research signals, not as guarantees or official statements. Always verify the current policy, quote, availability and destination terms on the live service before making a transaction.

## Map the marketplace journey

Sugargoo Spreadsheet resources can help organize products from multiple Chinese marketplaces. Start by confirming whether the current service accepts the link and item, then record seller, product, size and destination questions before moving to a live quote.

## Understand QC and rehearsal terms

Ask what QC images show, when they are available, what extra requests cost, and how rehearsal or repacking changes the parcel calculation. QC is a visual aid and rehearsal is a quote-estimation step; neither is a guarantee of authenticity or final delivery cost.

## Compare consolidated shipping carefully

When combining items, compare the selected route, parcel dimensions, chargeable weight, insurance, tracking and destination restrictions. Community praise for SAL or sea-mail may reflect a particular country, season and parcel, so verify a current quote for the actual destination.

## Build a repeatable shortlist

Keep a table of item links, seller notes, expected weight, packaging preference, route options and unresolved questions. This makes multi-platform shopping easier to review and reduces decisions based on a single testimonial.

## Questions to verify before you proceed

### Is SAL always the cheapest or fastest line?

No. Compare a current quote for the actual destination, parcel and service conditions.

### Do QC photos replace a product check?

No. They help review visible details but have limits and do not guarantee authenticity or future condition.

## Editorial note

This Sugargoo page is an informational resource. Community posts can be incomplete, outdated, misattributed or specific to one destination and order. Treat them as prompts for verification, not as proof of platform-wide performance.
